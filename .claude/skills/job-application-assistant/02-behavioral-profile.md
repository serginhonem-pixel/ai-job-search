# Behavioral Profile

## Overview
Sergio Betini's behavioral assessment identifies them as a **Technical Catalyst / Data-Driven Operator** pattern. A results-oriented leader who bridges the gap between raw shop floor execution and advanced data analytics to eliminate operational waste and build highly predictable supply chains.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Analytical Thinking | Very High | Natural tendency to approach problems through data, mathematical optimization (e.g., Cutting Stock), and systematic root-cause analysis. |
| Autonomy & Initiative | High | Self-starter who proactively builds tools (e.g., custom JavaScript apps, ERP automation) without waiting for corporate IT resources. |
| Leadership & Team Dev | High | Experienced in managing large teams (up to 40 people) by balancing tactical KPIs with safety and collaboration. |
| Process Orientation | Very High | Deeply focused on structuring workflow logic, mapping EDI integrations, and transitioning production strategies (MTO to MTS). |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Pragmatic Innovator:** Combines field knowledge with coding skills (JavaScript, Power BI) to automate away manual, time-consuming tasks.
- **Cost-Conscious Optimizer:** Constantly audits operational inputs, renegotiating vendor contracts and restructuring cutting methods to salvage margins.
- **Cross-Functional Bridge:** Smooths out friction between commercial demands and manufacturing capabilities by building centralized communication tools.

## How You Work Best
- **Data-Empowered Environments:** Where decisions are driven by clear performance metrics, KPIs, and integrated ERP systems (TOTVS, SAP, Sankhya).
- **High-Autonomy / Remote Setups:** Thrives in roles (including remote/hybrid) where they are given a problem (e.g., high scrap rates, material stockouts) and trusted to engineer the technical solution.
- **Complex Operations:** Environments with multi-variable challenges like mixed supply chains, fleet logistics, and industrial maintenance scheduling.

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Deep Technical Absorption:** Because of a strong drive to build technical solutions independently, may initially bypass existing software. *Framing:* Leverages personal development skills to rapidly prototype solutions while driving maximum adoption of core corporate enterprise systems.
- **High Standards for Data Maturity:** Can feel restricted by environments completely lacking data structure. *Framing:* Acts as a transformation agent, taking manual, spreadsheet-heavy operations and elevating them to automated, transparent Power BI systems.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "Otimização de processos", "Redução de custos", "S&OP", "Automação", "Cultura de dados", "Gestão de KPIs", "Sistemas ERP", "Tomada de decisão baseada em dados", "Liderança de equipes".

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "Processos 100% manuais", "Ambiente estritamente focado em tarefas rotineiras e sem espaço para inovação", "Microgestão extrema", "Ausência total de sistemas/ERP".

## Management Style Preferences
- **What works:** Management by objectives (MBO) where leadership defines the target metrics (e.g., reducing lead time, cutting scrap) and allows architectural freedom to deliver.
- **What doesn't work:** Rigid microgestão that stifles technical innovation or forces repetitive manual tasks that could otherwise be automated through code.

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Emphasize quantified savings (e.g., 20% scrap reduction, 78% supply cost cut) achieved through a mix of sharp leadership and custom data pipelines.
- **CV:** Highlight proficiency in tying production planning (PCP) directly to corporate financial health and technological scale.
- **Interviews:** Use the STAR method to explain how you engineered custom tools (like the Slitter optimizer or QtdApp) to solve real floor bottlenecks.
- **Don't overstate:** Do not claim to be a pure full-time software developer; you are an industrial manager who utilizes programming as a strategic superpower for industrial operations.
